How do I roll back a deployment?#

Open the deployment, choose "Roll back" and confirm. The previous version is restored within a minute and the event appears in the stream.

Why is my canary stuck at 10%?#

A canary holds until its error rate stays below the threshold for the configured window. Check the service's health signals.

Can I deploy to more than one environment at once?#

Not today. Promote through environments in order so each gate runs.
